We first launched Relisten in 2013<Footnote content="And its prior iteration, 'Listen to the Dead' in 2012" />. Our goal remains the same – to make the ever growing corpus of recorded live music as accessible as possible.

What began as an exclusively Grateful Dead streaming platform has now grown to support over 4,000 bands. Last month, over 2 million tracks were played on Relisten, cumulatively over 300,000 hours.

As Relisten has grown, we've put thousands of hours behind the scenes into maintaining and improving it. Most of that work is hidden from view. And though we've preferred to let our work speak for us, we also rather enjoy talking about the work we're doing. So in a world of cheap text, here's a blog of (hopefully) authentic writing where we can share some stories.

Relisten is completely non-commercial, open source, and free. We never had user accounts or logins because we genuinely didn't want your personal data. We're not a business - we're here for the love of the game.

Though we've recently unlocked some major new developments in which user accounts will become genuinely _useful_. The anonymous, logged out Relisten experience will continue to be of paramount importance, and the logged in experience will be completely optional and minimal.

As always, if you have any interest in contributing to the Relisten codebase, or you have some feedback for us, please stop by our [Discord](https://relisten.net/discord) and say hello!

Thanks for helping shape and use the platform for the past decade. Thank you the bands that permiss taping and trading, thank you the tapers and archivists who put in the altruistic effort, thank you to the platforms<Footnote content="archive.org and phish.in" /> who host the music, thank you to the listener – we are all links in a cohesive chain. Here's to the next one.
