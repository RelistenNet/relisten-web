We first launched Relisten in 2013<Footnote content="And its prior iteration, 'Listen to the Dead' in 2012" />. Our goal remains the same – to make the ever growing corpus of recorded live music as accessible as possible.

What began as an exclusively Grateful Dead streaming platform now supports over 4,000 artists. Last month, over 2 million tracks were played on Relisten, totalling in over 300,000 hours (12,500 days or the equivalent of ~100,000 Dead shows).

As Relisten has grown, we've put thousands of hours behind the scenes into maintaining and improving it. Most of that work is hidden from view. But we figured we'd catch up to the internet of 2002 to start a blog and share some stories.

Today we're also launching this new website, replete with a variety of features to make digging into recordings easier. In the last few months, we released a major mobile app update and a large Sonos app update. We grew our catalogue roughly 20 times over<Footnote content="And we have more artists coming" />.

We've also been knee deep in some major developments that are right around the bend. As always, if you have any interest in contributing to the Relisten codebase, or you have some feedback for us, please stop by our [Discord](https://relisten.net/discord) and say hello!

Thanks for helping shape and use the platform for the past decade. Thank you the bands that permiss taping and trading, thank you the tapers and archivists who put in the wholly altruistic effort, thank you to the platforms<Footnote content="like archive.org, phish.in, the spreadsheet, etree, and others" /> who host the music, and thank you to the listener – we are all links in a cohesive chain.
